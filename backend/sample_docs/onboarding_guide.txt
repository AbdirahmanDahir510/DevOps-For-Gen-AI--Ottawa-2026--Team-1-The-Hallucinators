New Employee Onboarding Guide

Day 1: Meet with your manager and complete HR paperwork.
Day 2: Set up your development environment and access company tools.
Day 3: Attend the security and compliance training session.
Week 1: Shadow a team member on active projects.
Week 2: Begin contributing to a small starter task assigned by your manager.
All new employees must complete security training within their first 5 business days.
