Company Remote Work Policy

Employees are permitted to work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Remote work requests must be submitted at least 48 hours in advance through the HR portal.
All employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours, 10am to 3pm local time.
Equipment provided for remote work includes a laptop and a monitor, issued by the IT department.
Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure home network when accessing company systems.
